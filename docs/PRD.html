Product requirements document

PGCity

PG Discovery & Enrollment Platform

v2.0 · Final, reviewed Ready for engineering & design handoff 16 August 2026
MVP in one line

A user can find a nearby PG on a map, explore it richly, unlock its contact number via a rewarded ad, and submit a verified enrollment request — all without re-typing their details after the first time.

PGCity is a mobile app (Android first, iOS later) that helps students and working professionals discover Paying Guest (PG) accommodation on an interactive map, explore each PG through a dedicated PG Web Screen (photos, 360° tour, video, amenities, rules, pricing), and enroll with a saved profile instead of re-entering personal details for every property.

The MVP uses a curated model: only the PGCity team can onboard and publish PGs. Contact numbers are monetized through a rewarded-ad unlock; enrollment is a separate, higher-intent conversion path that captures a lead PGCity can later hand to a future PG Owner App.

Why now

  • PG search today is fragmented across brokers, WhatsApp groups, and generic listing apps with poor, unverified media.
  • A map-first, per-PG "mini website" experience is a differentiated wedge versus flat listing directories.
  • The architecture is intentionally property-agnostic so PGCity can expand into rental homes, flats, and hostels without a rebuild (Section 33, Future Roadmap).
This is the original PGCity PRD with gaps closed and structure normalized. Sections tagged NEW were absent from the original draft and were added because they're typically required before a PRD is build-ready — regulatory posture, measurable goals, explicit scope boundaries, and a risk register are the most consequential additions.

PGCity is a location-based accommodation discovery application that helps users find nearby PG accommodations through an interactive map. Unlike a traditional property-listing app, every onboarded PG gets a dedicated PG Web Screen — a mini website inside the app.

Core user capabilities

  • Discover nearby PGs on a map
  • Search PGs by name or area
  • Filter PGs by gender and price
  • Open a complete PG profile
  • View 360°/3D tours
  • Watch PG-specific videos/vlogs
  • View PG information, photos and location
  • Like PGs
  • Request/contact the PG
  • Enroll/apply for a PG
  • Share PG listings
  • Maintain a saved profile to avoid repeated data entry

PGCity will initially use a curated listing model, meaning only the PGCity team can onboard and publish PGs. A future version will give PG owners a separate app to manage their PG and view enrollment requests.

Problem statement

People searching for PG accommodation today typically have to:

  • Search across multiple platforms
  • Contact multiple PG owners individually
  • Visit properties physically before understanding what they actually offer
  • Depend on incomplete or misleading photographs
  • Ask the same questions repeatedly (rules, deposit, food, sharing)
  • Verify manually whether a PG suits their gender, budget and location
  • Re-enter the same personal information when contacting different PGs

PGCity aims to simplify this by providing a rich, verified, and centralized PG discovery experience.

Product vision

"Find your perfect PG nearby, explore it virtually, and enroll with confidence."

PGCity should make finding a PG feel more like browsing a modern property website than browsing a simple listing directory.

Product principle

PGCity should not be positioned as another PG listing application. Its differentiation is: "Every PG has its own digital experience." The combination of map discovery, curated listings, dedicated PG Web Screens, 360° tours, PG-specific videos, verified information, contact unlocking, one-click enrollment, and deep sharing creates a stronger experience than a basic property directory.

NEW

Added — a PRD that claims differentiation should name what it is differentiating from. PGCity competes with generic property-listing apps/portals and informal channels (brokers, WhatsApp/Telegram groups, college hostel boards). Most incumbents optimize for listing volume, not listing depth.

This is not a claim that PGCity is superior on every axis (e.g. informal channels can be faster for a single known PG) — it is the basis for where PGCity should invest first: media richness and enrollment tracking, not raw listing count.

Scope

8.1 MVP scope (in)

The first version will contain two primary user-facing screens: Home Screen and PG Web Screen. Supporting functionality includes: user profile, authentication/OTP, enrollment form, likes, contact unlock, PG sharing, deep linking, notifications, backend database, and an admin PG onboarding system.

8.2 Explicitly out of scope for MVP

NEW

Added — the original PRD listed roadmap phases but never stated what is deliberately excluded from v1, which is what actually protects the launch date.

  • PG Owner App (Phase 2) — owners do not get any login or dashboard in MVP; all onboarding is admin-only.
  • Online booking, payments, or digital agreements (Phase 4).
  • Reviews & ratings (Phase 4).
  • Any property type other than PG (rental homes, flats, hostels, co-living are Phase 3).
  • Advanced filters beyond gender and simple price bucket (AC, food, Wi-Fi, sharing type, etc. — Section 11.4.3).
  • Room-level / bed-level availability.
  • Google/Apple social sign-in (mobile OTP only for MVP).
  • In-app chat between user and PG owner (contact is via phone number unlock only).
  • iOS app (tracked separately, not blocking Android MVP launch).

Assumptions & constraints

NEW

9.1 Assumptions

  • PGCity's internal team (not owners) will have the capacity to manually onboard and keep at least 150 PGs updated at launch in the first city.
  • Users are willing to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock a contact number in exchange for free discovery.
  • A single launch city is sufficient to validate the model before multi-city expansion.
  • Mobile number + OTP is an acceptable sole identity mechanism for the target audience (largely true in the Indian market).

9.2 Constraints

  • Curated-only onboarding means supply growth is bounded by admin team capacity, not self-serve owner growth, until Phase 2.
  • No payment processing in MVP — enrollment is a lead-generation event only, not a booking.
  • Android-only at launch limits addressable audience versus iOS-inclusive competitors.
  • PG contact numbers must never be exposed in any public/unauthenticated API response (Section 21, Security).

Core experience

Application navigation

Recommended bottom navigation: Home | Liked | Profile.

  • Home — main PG discovery experience.
  • Liked — PGs the user previously liked.
  • Profile — user information and enrollment-related information.

The Liked tab can be omitted from the first MVP if development scope needs to remain minimal.

Home screen

11.1 Purpose

The Home Screen is the primary PG discovery interface.

11.2 Map

The main area of the screen should contain an interactive map similar to Google Maps, displaying: the user's current location, nearby PG markers, PG name/short label, optional price information, and the selected PG marker state.

Example marker label: ₹8,500 — Sunrise PG.

When a user taps a PG marker, a small preview card should appear containing: PG name, PG type, starting price, distance, rating/likes, thumbnail, and a "View PG" button.

11.3 Search

A search bar should be displayed prominently above the map. Placeholder: "Search PG name or area."

Users should be able to search by PG name, area/locality, landmark, or nearby location — e.g. "Sunrise PG," "Maninagar," "Satellite," "Near XYZ College." Search results should dynamically update the map.

11.4 Filters

11.4.1 Gender filter

Options: All, Girls PG, Boys PG. Future: Co-living.

11.4.2 Price filter

MVP should provide simple price filtering: Under ₹10,000 and Above ₹10,000. Future versions can introduce finer buckets (Under ₹5,000; ₹5,000–₹10,000; ₹10,000–₹15,000; ₹15,000–₹20,000; ₹20,000+) and eventually a custom price range slider.

11.4.3 Additional filters (post-MVP)

  • Distance
  • AC / Non-AC
  • Food included
  • Wi-Fi
  • Laundry
  • Attached bathroom
  • Furnished
  • Parking
  • Security
  • Single / double / triple sharing
  • Availability

11.5 PG marker / preview card

Tapping a marker shows: PG name, Girls PG / Boys PG, price (e.g. ₹8,500/month), distance (e.g. 1.2 km away), a thumbnail image, and a "View PG →" control that opens the PG Web Screen.

PG Web Screen

The PG Web Screen is the most important differentiating feature of PGCity. Each PG should have a dedicated page that feels like a personal website for that PG.

12.1 Structure (recommended order)

  1. Header
  2. 360° / 3D tour
  3. PG video/vlog
  4. Quick information
  5. PG description
  6. Amenities
  7. Pricing
  8. Rules
  9. Location
  10. Photos
  11. Likes
  12. Contact
  13. Enroll
  14. Share

12.2 PG header

Displays: PG name (large typography, e.g. "Sunrise Girls PG"); below the name — Girls PG, Ahmedabad, ₹8,500/month, Available / Currently Full; optional Verified PG badge.

12.3 360° / 3D virtual tour

A large rounded rectangular card displays the PG's virtual tour ("Explore PG in 360°") with a preview image, 360° icon, and "Explore 360°" button. Clicking opens a full-screen immersive viewer. Users should be able to rotate the view, look around the room, explore different areas, zoom where supported, and move between supported viewpoints.

Future enhancement: multiple locations (Entrance, Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom, Common area, Terrace, Study area).

12.4 PG video / YouTube vlog

Below the 360° section: a rounded rectangular video card ("Take a Video Tour"). The video may be hosted on YouTube; clicking the card opens it. Ideally PG-specific, e.g. "Complete Sunrise PG Tour | Ahmedabad."

12.6 PG description

A detailed description should explain the property, e.g.: "Sunrise Girls PG provides fully furnished accommodation with Wi-Fi, food, laundry and 24/7 security. The property is located close to XYZ College and major transportation facilities." The description should support rich but controlled content.

12.7 Amenities

Displayed using icons. Only amenities configured by the PGCity Admin should appear. Possible amenities:

  • Wi-Fi
  • Food
  • AC
  • Washing machine
  • Laundry
  • Parking
  • CCTV
  • 24/7 security
  • Power backup
  • Hot water
  • Attached bathroom
  • Study table
  • Refrigerator
  • Common room

12.8 PG rules

Each PG should clearly display rules, for example:

  • No smoking
  • No alcohol
  • Entry timing
  • Visitor policy
  • Minimum stay
  • Deposit requirements
  • Notice period
  • Other PG-specific rules

This helps users understand requirements before enrollment.

12.9 Location

Includes: map, PG address, nearby landmarks, distance from user, nearby college/office, transportation information. Example "Nearby" list: XYZ College — 1.2 km, Metro Station — 800 m, Main Market — 500 m. A "Get Directions" button can open the user's preferred map application.

12.10 PG photos

Supports a maximum of 10 photos in MVP, covering: exterior, bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, dining area, common area, study area, balcony, building, and other important areas. Users can tap a photo to open a full-screen gallery.

12.11 Like feature

Users should be able to like a PG (e.g. ❤️ 1,284 Likes). Tapping again removes the like. The backend stores the User ↔ PG relationship to prevent one user from artificially inflating the count by repeated tapping.

12.12 Contact feature

The PG Web Screen has a "View Contact" button. The phone number is not immediately visible.

Monetization mechanism

The user must watch a rewarded advertisement before the contact number is revealed. Flow: View Contact → Watch Ad to Unlock Contact → Rewarded Ad → Contact number becomes visible (e.g. 📞 +91 XXXXX XXXXX). Exact ad frequency and eligibility should be controlled from backend configuration.

Contact abuse prevention

  • Contact number should not be stored directly in public PG listing data
  • Contact should be revealed only after successful reward validation
  • Backend should record contact unlock events
  • Optional daily unlock limits can be introduced
  • Admin should be able to disable contact visibility for a PG

12.13 Enrollment feature

The main CTA is "Enroll Now." If the user is not logged in, this opens the enrollment/login flow. If already logged in, it displays saved information.

Enrollment form

Personal information: Full Name, Mobile Number, Email, Gender, Age, Date of Birth (optional). Accommodation information: preferred move-in date, preferred sharing type, expected duration, occupation, college/company name. Optional: current city, emergency contact, additional message. The exact information should be minimized to what is actually required by PG owners.

Mobile number verification

Flow: Enter Mobile Number → Send OTP → Enter OTP → Verify → Account Verified → Complete Enrollment Profile.

Saved user profile

Once the enrollment profile is completed, information is saved. Next time: Enroll Now → show saved information → user confirms/updates details → Submit Enrollment.

Enrollment confirmation

Before submission, display a summary — e.g. "You are enrolling for: Sunrise Girls PG," Applicant: Yuvraj, Move-in Date: 15 September 2026, Sharing: 2 Sharing — then "Submit Enrollment Request."

Enrollment request status

After submission: ✅ Enrollment Request Sent. The user should see PG name, request date, and status. Possible statuses: Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled. This becomes especially useful once the future PG Owner App launches.

12.14 Share PG

The PG Web Screen should contain a "Share PG" button that generates a universal/deep link such as pgcity.app/pg/sunrise-girls-pg (final domain TBD).

Deep linking behavior

Case 1 — PGCity installed: user clicks the shared link → PGCity opens → the specific PG Web Screen opens directly (e.g. "Sunrise Girls PG").

Case 2 — PGCity not installed: user clicks link → website/landing page opens → user is redirected to the appropriate store (Android → Google Play, iPhone → App Store). After installation, the app should ideally open the originally shared PG — this requires deferred deep linking.

My enrollments

15.1 PG availability

Every PG should have an availability status: Available, Limited Availability, Full, Temporarily Unavailable. This prevents users from submitting requests for unavailable PGs. Future enhancement: room-level availability, e.g. "2 Sharing — 3 beds available."

15.2 PG verification system

Since PGCity controls onboarding, every PG should have an internal status:

  • Draft — admin is preparing listing
  • Pending Verification — information being checked
  • Verified — PG approved
  • Published — visible to users
  • Suspended — temporarily hidden
  • Archived — permanently removed from discovery

A Verified PG badge can increase user trust.

15.4 Product decision — contact vs enrollment

PGCity should treat these as two separate conversion actions. Contact: the user wants to quickly speak with the PG — Watch ad → Unlock phone number. Enroll: the user wants to formally submit interest — Verify phone → Fill/confirm profile → Submit enrollment. This separation makes the user journey clearer.

Authentication

Primary: mobile number + OTP. Optional future: Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In. The user's mobile number should be uniquely associated with their account.

Notifications

The application should support push notifications, e.g.:

  • Enrollment — "Your enrollment request for Sunrise Girls PG has been received."
  • Owner response — "Sunrise Girls PG has accepted your enrollment request."
  • Availability — "A PG you liked now has availability."

Notifications should be opt-in/permission-controlled where required.

IV

Trust & compliance

Security, privacy & compliance

21.1 Security requirements

Sensitive information — phone numbers, email, applicant details, enrollment information — must not be publicly exposed. Backend access should use authenticated APIs/database rules.

  • Role-based access
  • User authentication
  • Admin authentication
  • Owner authentication (future)
  • Secure OTP verification
  • Server-side authorization
  • Input validation
  • Rate limiting
  • Abuse prevention

21.2 Privacy

Because PGCity collects personal information, the application should provide a Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, a data deletion mechanism, account deletion, and consent where appropriate. Users should know why their enrollment information is collected and who receives it.

21.3 Regulatory compliance

NEW

Added — the original PRD covered privacy in principle but did not name the applicable law or specific obligations it creates for an India-first launch.

As an India-first product handling personal data (name, phone, email, gender, age, move-in intent), PGCity is a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). At minimum, the product should:

  • Present clear notice of what personal data is collected and why, before or at the point of collection (enrollment form, OTP signup).
  • Obtain explicit, itemized consent — not a single bundled checkbox — where feasible, and let users withdraw consent as easily as they gave it.
  • Support a verifiable data-deletion/erasure request from within the app (ties to Profile → Delete Account, Section 13).
  • Avoid sharing enrollment data with a PG/owner until Phase 2's Owner App has its own compliant data-handling flow.
  • Define a data retention period for unlocked-contact and enrollment records rather than retaining indefinitely.
This is not legal advice; legal counsel should review the final consent copy and retention schedule before launch.

Accessibility

NEW

Added — not covered in the original document.

  • Support Android system font-scaling up to at least 130% without breaking PG Web Screen layouts.
  • Minimum touch target size of 44x44dp for all interactive elements (Like, Enroll, filter chips, map markers).
  • Sufficient color contrast (WCAG AA, 4.5:1) for price/availability text overlaid on photos or map markers.
  • Content descriptions for TalkBack on all icon-only controls (heart/like, share, 360° icon, filter icons).
  • Captions or a text transcript link is not required for owner-hosted YouTube videos, but the PG description should never be the only source of critical facts (rules, price) that also appear only in a video.

Localization & language

NEW
  • MVP ships in English only.
  • UI strings should be externalized (not hard-coded) from day one so Hindi and the launch city's regional language can be added post-MVP without a rework.
  • Currency and number formatting should follow Indian numbering conventions (₹8,500 not $8,500 or 8500.00) throughout.
  • Address and location search should tolerate both English and transliterated local-language place names (e.g. "Maninagar" and common alternate spellings).

Non-functional requirements

24.1 Performance

  • Home screen should load quickly.
  • Map should avoid loading unnecessary PG data.
  • Images should be optimized.
  • Lazy loading should be used for PG photos.
  • PG details should load progressively.

24.2 Reliability

  • Enrollment requests must not be lost.
  • OTP verification should handle retry scenarios.
  • Network failures should provide clear messages.

24.3 Security

  • Authentication required for personal information.
  • Personal information must not be publicly accessible.
  • Admin APIs must be protected.

24.4 Scalability

The backend should support expansion from 100 PGs → 1,000 PGs → 10,000+ PGs without requiring a major architecture redesign.

Empty & error states

25.1 Empty states

Example: "No PGs Found." Try changing: Location, Price filter, Gender filter, Search area. Button: "Clear Filters."

25.2 Error states

  • Location unavailable — "We couldn't access your location." Button: "Enable Location."
  • Network unavailable — "Check your internet connection and try again."
  • PG unavailable — "This PG is currently unavailable."
  • Enrollment failure — "We couldn't submit your enrollment request. Please try again."

Support & help

NEW

Added — the original PRD had no path for a confused or blocked user to get help.

  • An in-app "Help / Contact Us" entry point should exist from the Profile screen, at minimum offering a support email or WhatsApp link.
  • A short FAQ should cover the two moments users are most likely to be confused: why a contact number requires watching an ad, and what "Under Review" on an enrollment status means.
  • Enrollment-status screens should not be a dead end — if a request sits in "Submitted" beyond a reasonable window (e.g. 5 business days) with no admin/owner action, the UI should surface a "Need help with this?" prompt rather than leaving the user guessing.

Backend architecture

A scalable architecture could contain: Mobile App (Flutter) → Authentication (OTP) → Backend API (handles PGs, Users, Likes, Enrollment, Contact unlocks, Analytics) → Database (Users, PGs, Enrollments, Likes, Contact unlock records, Admin data) → Storage (PG photos, 360° assets, other media) → External Services (Maps, YouTube, OTP provider, Push notifications, Advertising platform). The exact backend technology can be finalized during technical design.

29.1 Map technology

The map should support: current location, PG markers, marker clustering, map zoom, search movement, selected PG, and distance calculation. The exact map provider can be selected during technical architecture.

29.2 Search and discovery logic

The backend should support PG name search, area search, location-based search, gender filtering, price filtering, and availability filtering. Future: amenity filtering, distance sorting, popularity sorting, recently added, recommended PGs.

33.1 Future rental home expansion

The architecture should not hard-code the product around PGs only. Instead, introduce a generic property concept — Property Type: PG, Rental Home, Flat, Hostel, Co-living — allowing PGCity to evolve into a broader accommodation platform without rebuilding the entire application.

33.2 Recommended future features

  • Ratings — e.g. 4.5 ⭐.

Success metrics & definition of MVP success

The primary goal is not simply app downloads. PGCity should measure:

Discovery

  • Searches/user
  • PG views/user
  • Map interactions

Engagement

  • Likes
  • Video views
  • 360° tour views
  • Shares

Lead generation

  • Contact unlocks
  • Enrollment starts
  • Completed enrollments

Conversion

  • PG View → Contact
  • PG View → Enrollment
  • Enrollment → Accepted

These metrics indicate whether PGCity is actually helping users find accommodation. See Section 5 for the specific 90-day launch targets.

34.1 Definition of MVP success

PGCity MVP will be considered successful when a new user can:

  1. Open the application.
  2. Discover nearby PGs.
  3. Search or filter PGs.
  4. Open a PG's dedicated page.
  5. Understand the PG's pricing, amenities, rules and location.
  6. Explore its media/virtual tour.
  7. Like or share the PG.
  8. Unlock its contact through the defined ad flow.
  9. Verify their phone number.
  10. Complete their profile.
  11. Submit an enrollment request.
  12. View the submitted request in their profile.
  13. Allow the PGCity backend/admin system to receive and manage the lead.

Long-term vision

PGCity can eventually become a complete accommodation discovery and lead-management ecosystem: User App (discover PGs and rental homes) → PG Owner App (manage properties and applicants) → Admin Dashboard (manage the marketplace and verification) → Property Owners (receive qualified leads) → Users (Discover → Explore → Contact → Enroll → Book). The initial PG-only model should therefore be built with a property-agnostic backend architecture, making future rental-home expansion much easier.

36.1 Future PG Owner App integration

PGCity should be designed from the beginning so enrollment requests can be consumed by a separate PG Owner application. Architecture: PGCity User App → Backend → PG Owner App. The owner can eventually see applicant name, phone number, email, move-in date, sharing preference, applicant message, enrollment timestamp, and status, and update Pending → Accepted / Rejected. The user application should receive the status update.

Glossary

NEW
PG
Paying Guest accommodation — a room or bed rented out, typically with shared common facilities.
PG Web Screen
The dedicated, rich in-app profile page for a single PG — PGCity's core differentiator.
Curated listing model
Only the PGCity internal team can create/publish PG listings; owners cannot self-list in MVP.
Contact unlock
The rewarded-ad-gated action that reveals a PG's phone number to the user.
Enrollment
A user's formal application of interest in a specific PG, distinct from simply unlocking contact.
Deferred deep link
A link that, after app install, still routes the user to the specific content (PG) originally shared.

A listing that has passed the admin's Pending Verification review step.
